During the summer of 2024, I had the opportunity to become a web design intern at Phoenix College. During this time, I collaborated with a team to craft a website aimed at teaching software development to children. Geared towards the demographic of kids in 5th through 8th grade, the foundations of computer programming were wrapped up in an easy-to-digest package. By integrating games and interactive elements, while also keeping color theory, accessibility, and tech justice at the forefront, a high-fidelity wireframe was put together in Figma and presented to a panel of mentors and peers.
